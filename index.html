01
Case Study

SuperYou

From protein wafers to a high-growth consumer brand.

SuperYou is a Mumbai-based protein-food brand co-created by Nikunj Biyani and Ranveer Singh. Its proposition is simple: put protein into familiar snack formats so nutrition can fit into everyday routines. SuperYou's own site describes its wafers as delivering 10g protein per serving and positions protein as an everyday, not purely athletic, need.

Product: familiar format, functional benefit

SuperYou's standard wafer is positioned as a snack that combines indulgent flavours with 10g protein. The company also now offers 20g Mega wafers, chips, protein powders and creatine.

03
Learning & Development

Build the habit,
not just the product.

A startup does not become a business simply because customers like the product once. The SuperYou case can be used to understand the path from problem-solution fit to repeatable demand.

Mini masterclass

From idea to repeat purchase

Use this four-step framework to evaluate any consumer startup. The SuperYou case gives a concrete example at every stage.

01

Find the friction

What existing behaviour is inconvenient, expensive or underserved?

SuperYou: protein can feel like a separate “fitness” task.

02

Change the format

Can the solution fit naturally into an existing habit?

SuperYou: turn protein into a familiar snack.

03

Engineer distribution

How will customers discover, try and re-buy it?

SuperYou: build across digital and offline channels.

04

Measure the loop

What tells you whether a product is becoming a habit?

Watch: repeat purchase, CAC, contribution margin and channel velocity.

THE STARTUP TEST

Would the customer miss you if you disappeared?

If the answer is no, the startup may have achieved trial without yet achieving habit. That distinction is where product-market fit becomes a business model question.
